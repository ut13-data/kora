# Communication Tone Profile — Germany (Munich, Berlin offices)

*This describes common workplace tendencies observed in Nexora's German offices, not a
rule that applies to every individual. Individual preferences always take precedence —
see DOC-COMM-001.*

- **Directness:** A common workplace tendency toward explicit, direct requests
  ("Please send X by Friday" rather than "It would be great if we could look at X at
  some point").
- **Formality:** Moderate; first names are standard once introduced, but written
  communication tends to stay professional and to-the-point rather than casual.
- **Disagreement:** Often communicated relatively directly and early, sometimes framed
  as a factual objection rather than softened — this is not typically intended as
  personal criticism.
- **Feedback:** Tends to be specific and given fairly directly, often separated clearly
  from social/personal rapport ("professional clarity" is valued independent of personal
  friendliness).
- **Urgency:** Often communicated explicitly with a stated deadline rather than implied
  through tone or repetition.
- **Meetings:** Tend to start on time, follow an agenda, and end with clear action items
  and owners.
- **Email/message conventions:** Relatively concise; long preambles before the actual
  request are less common.

## What May Unintentionally Land Wrong

- **Too indirect:** A vague request without a clear deadline or owner may be read as
  unclear rather than as polite, and may need to be re-asked more explicitly.
- **Too direct (in the other direction):** Extremely blunt feedback with no acknowledgment
  of effort can still land poorly here too — directness is not the same as bluntness
  without context.
- **Appropriate example:** "The report needs an update to section 3 — can you send a
  revised version by Thursday?" (clear ask, clear deadline, professional tone).

## Individual Preference Reminder
Not every colleague based in Munich or Berlin is German, and not every German colleague
matches this profile. Ask, or notice how a specific colleague actually communicates, and
adapt accordingly.
