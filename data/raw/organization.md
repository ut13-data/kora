# Organization Structure

Nexora Systems is organized into 12 departments, each led by a department head who
reports to the CEO or, for Engineering/Product/Data, to the CTO.

## Reporting Lines (simplified)

```
CEO
├── CTO
│   ├── VP Engineering → Engineering Managers → Software Engineers / QA / DevOps
│   ├── VP Product → Product Managers → Product Analysts
│   └── Data & Analytics Manager → Data Analysts / Data Engineers / BI Developers
├── VP Sales
│   └── Sales Managers → Account Executives / Sales Development Reps
├── VP Marketing → Marketing Managers → Marketing Specialists
├── VP Customer Success
│   ├── Customer Success Managers
│   └── Implementation Manager → Implementation Consultants / Solutions Architects
├── Support Team Lead → Support Engineers (L1/L2/L3)
├── CFO
│   └── Finance Manager → Accountants / Financial Analysts / AP-AR Specialists
├── Head of HR
│   └── HR Managers → HR Business Partners / HR Generalists / Recruiters
├── CISO
│   └── IT Manager → IT Support Specialists / Security Analysts
└── Operations Manager → Operations Analysts / Office Managers
```

## Note on Titles — Known Inconsistency

As of the FY2025 reorganization (effective 2025-03-01), the title **"Implementation
Manager"** replaced the legacy title **"Project Lead"** for the person who owns customer
go-live sign-off within the Implementation/Consulting department. Some older SOPs
(pre-2025) have not yet been updated and still refer to "Project Lead." Treat
"Project Lead" in any document dated before 2025-03-01 as equivalent to "Implementation
Manager" unless the document has since been revised. See PROC-IMPL-001 v1.0 vs v2.0 for
an example of this exact inconsistency.
