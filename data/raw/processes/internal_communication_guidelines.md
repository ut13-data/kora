# Internal Communication Guidelines

## Channels
- **Chat (internal messaging tool)** — Default for quick questions, day-to-day
  coordination. Response expectation: same working day.
- **Email** — Formal communications, external-facing content, anything requiring a
  documented record.
- **Video calls** — Default for cross-timezone discussions and anything requiring
  real-time back-and-forth (Munich–Bengaluru overlap is limited; see DOC-DEPT-PROD).

## General Principles
- State the ask clearly, including any deadline, in the first two sentences of a
  message.
- Copy the relevant manager on decisions that affect budget, headcount, or customer
  commitments.
- Prefer asynchronous written updates over meetings where a meeting isn't strictly
  necessary — Nexora is a multi-timezone company and meetings have a real cost for
  someone's evening or early morning.

## Cross-Cultural Note
These are company-wide baseline expectations. How individuals *phrase* requests,
give feedback, or signal urgency may reasonably vary by cultural background and personal
style — see communication_culture.md for guidance on adapting tone (not substance) across
Nexora's office locations.
