# Proposal and Quotation Process

## Purpose
Standardize how pricing proposals are built, approved, and delivered to prospects.

## Steps

1. **Scoping call** — AE confirms which products, tiers, and (if applicable) custom
   development or complex integration needs apply. Complex integration scope requires a
   Solutions Architect review before quoting (see DOC-PROD-NEXCONNECT).
2. **Quote generation** — AE builds the quote in NexCRM using standard pricing tables.
3. **Discount approval** — Discounts up to 15% require no additional approval. 15–30%
   requires Sales Manager approval. Above 30%, or any multi-year term, requires VP Sales
   approval.
4. **Proposal delivery** — AE sends the proposal (quote + SOW) to the prospect, typically
   with a 30-day validity window.
5. **Contract signature** — Once signed, AE triggers the "Deal Closed" handoff record for
   Implementation (see PROC-IMPL-001, Step 1).

## Known Gap
This process does not currently specify what happens if a customer requests
mid-negotiation changes to committed go-live dates after a proposal has already been
approved at a given discount tier. In practice this is resolved ad hoc between the AE and
Sales Manager; no formal escalation path exists.
