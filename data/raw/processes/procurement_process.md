# Procurement Process

## Purpose
Govern how Nexora purchases goods/services from external vendors (software licenses,
office equipment, contracted services).

## Steps

1. **Request** — Requester submits a purchase request via the internal procurement tool,
   including business justification.
2. **Manager approval** — Direct manager approves.
3. **Threshold approval** — Purchases €1,000–€10,000 require Finance Manager approval;
   above €10,000 requires CFO approval.
4. **Vendor setup** — Operations Analyst confirms the vendor is set up in the finance
   system (new vendors require a basic due-diligence check).
5. **Purchase order issuance** — Finance issues a PO; vendor invoices are matched against
   the PO at payment time (see PROC-FIN-003).

## Recurring Software Subscriptions
Department heads may approve recurring software subscriptions under €200/month directly
without going through the full procurement flow, but must still log the subscription
with Operations for the annual software audit.
