# Data Security Policy

## Scope
Applies to all Nexora employees, contractors, and any system handling customer or
employee data.

## Key Requirements

1. **Mandatory Security Training** — All employees must complete annual security
   awareness training. See PROC-HR-001 (Employee Onboarding) for the new-hire deadline —
   note there is a known version discrepancy between HR onboarding materials on this
   deadline; this document (PROC-IT-002) does not itself set the deadline, HR does.
2. **Customer Data Handling** — Customer production data may only be accessed by
   employees with an approved, logged business reason (see PROC-IT-001).
3. **Device Security** — Company laptops must have disk encryption and endpoint
   protection enabled; personal devices may not access customer production data.
4. **Incident Reporting** — Any suspected data exposure must be reported to the CISO
   within 24 hours of discovery, regardless of confirmed severity (see PROC-SUP-002).
5. **Data Retention** — Customer data is retained per the signed contract's data
   retention terms; default is 90 days post-contract-termination unless otherwise
   specified.

## Annual Review
This policy is reviewed annually by the CISO and Legal counsel (external).
