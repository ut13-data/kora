# Leave and HR Policies

## Annual Leave
- Germany-based employees (LOC-MUC, LOC-BER): 28 days/year, per German statutory
  minimum plus company standard.
- India-based employees (LOC-BLR): 24 days/year plus public holidays per Karnataka
  state calendar.
- US-based employees (LOC-AUS): 20 days/year (company policy; no federal statutory
  minimum applies).
- UK-based employees (LOC-LON): 25 days/year plus UK public holidays.

## Sick Leave
Location-specific, following local statutory requirements; HR Generalist can confirm
exact entitlement for a given employee's location and contract type.

## Parental Leave
Location-specific statutory entitlements apply as a floor; HR can provide country-
specific detail on request. This document does not itself enumerate country-specific
parental leave detail — check current official guidance for your location, or contact
your HR Generalist.

## Requesting Leave
All leave is requested and approved through the HR self-service portal; manager approval
required for leave exceeding 5 consecutive working days.
