# Customer Onboarding Process (ARCHIVED — superseded by PROC-IMPL-001 v2.0, effective
2025-03-01)

## Purpose
Move a customer from signed contract to live production usage.

## Steps

1. Kickoff call scheduled by Implementation Consultant.
2. Discovery and configuration.
3. Data migration.
4. Training.
5. **Project Lead** provides final go-live sign-off.
6. Handoff to Account Management (note: the "Account Management" function referenced
   here was reorganized into the Customer Success department in 2024; this document was
   not updated to reflect that change prior to being superseded).

*This version is retained for historical reference only. Do not follow this SOP — see
current version PROC-IMPL-001 v2.0.*
