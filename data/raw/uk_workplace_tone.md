# Communication Tone Profile — United Kingdom (London office)

*Common workplace tendency, not a rule for every individual — see DOC-COMM-001.*

- **Directness:** Requests are often softened with polite hedging ("Would you mind
  taking a look at...") even when there's a firm underlying deadline — the actual
  urgency may need to be stated explicitly rather than inferred from tone.
- **Formality:** Moderate; polite phrasing is common even in casual chat messages.
- **Disagreement:** Sometimes communicated indirectly, via understatement ("I wonder if
  there might be a small issue with...") rather than a flat statement of disagreement.
- **Feedback:** Often wrapped in polite framing; direct criticism may be softened enough
  that its seriousness isn't immediately obvious to someone unfamiliar with the style.
- **Urgency:** Because requests are often politely phrased, it's worth explicitly
  stating a deadline rather than relying on tone to convey time pressure.
- **Meetings:** Generally structured and punctual; humor and understatement are common
  even in fairly serious discussions.

## What May Unintentionally Land Wrong
- **Too indirect:** A softly-phrased concern can be misread as a minor comment rather
  than the real issue it's raising.
- **Too direct:** Very blunt phrasing, without any softening, can read as unnecessarily
  harsh even if the content itself is reasonable.
- **Appropriate example:** "Really appreciate the work on this — one thing worth
  flagging: could we get an updated version by Thursday?"

## Individual Preference Reminder
London houses Sales and Marketing at Nexora; individual style varies significantly, and
this profile should be treated as a light prior, not a fixed expectation.
