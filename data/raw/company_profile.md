# Nexora Systems

## Who We Are

Nexora Systems GmbH is a business-to-business software company that builds analytics,
operations, CRM, automation and data integration products for non-technical industries —
pharmaceutical distributors, manufacturers, retailers and logistics companies. We were
founded in 2011 in Munich, Germany, by three former ERP consultants (Lena Vogt, Rainer
Huber, and Priya Nair) who were frustrated that mid-sized industrial and distribution
companies were being sold enterprise software far too complex for their teams to use.

Nexora is privately held, profitable, and employs approximately 130 people across four
office locations. We are not a startup chasing hypergrowth; we are a steady, mid-sized
software vendor with long customer relationships (average customer tenure: 4.1 years) and
a reputation for "boring, reliable software that actually gets used."
