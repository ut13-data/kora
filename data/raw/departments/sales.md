# Sales Department

The Sales department is responsible for converting inbound and outbound interest into
signed contracts. It is split into two motions: New Business (net-new clients) and
Expansion (existing account upsell, handled jointly with Customer Success).

Sales is headquartered in Austin (LOC-AUS) for North America coverage, with a second
pod in London (LOC-LON) covering EMEA. There is no dedicated APAC sales pod; APAC deals
are currently handled remotely by the Austin team.

## Roles

**Sales Development Representative (SDR)** — Qualifies inbound leads, runs initial
discovery calls, books meetings for Account Executives. Entry-level role, typically
promoted to AE within 12–18 months.

**Account Executive (AE)** — Owns the deal from qualified lead through signed contract.
Responsible for the proposal and quotation process (PROC-SALES-002) and for the accuracy
of information handed to Implementation at deal close.

**Sales Manager** — Manages a pod of 4–6 AEs/SDRs, owns forecast accuracy, is the
approval authority for non-standard discounting above 15%.

**VP Sales** — Owns the department's revenue target, sits on the executive leadership
team, approves discounting above 30% and all multi-year contract terms.

## Handoff to Implementation

When a deal closes, the AE completes a "Deal Closed" handoff record containing the
signed SOW, agreed go-live date, and any custom commitments made during the sales cycle.
This handoff record is the single most common source of downstream confusion when
incomplete — see PROC-IMPL-001, Known Gap KG-01.
