# Data & Analytics Department

Data & Analytics is based in Bengaluru (LOC-BLR) and serves two audiences: internal
(company KPI reporting for leadership) and product (the analytics logic that powers
NexView Analytics for customers). This dual mandate is a frequent source of prioritization
tension between the "internal reporting" backlog and the "product analytics" backlog.

## Roles

**Data Analyst** — Builds internal dashboards and ad hoc analysis for Sales, Finance,
and Customer Success stakeholders.

**Data Engineer** — Builds and maintains data pipelines feeding both internal reporting
and NexView Analytics; owns data quality checks.

**BI Developer** — Builds and maintains customer-facing report templates within NexView
Analytics.

**Data & Analytics Manager** — Owns team prioritization and is the approval authority
for new production access to the analytics data warehouse (see PROC-IT-001).

## Access Note

New Data Analysts require Data & Analytics Manager approval plus IT provisioning to
reach the production analytics environment — see PROC-IT-001, "Analytics Environment
Access."
