# Communication Tone Profile — India (Bengaluru office)

*This describes common workplace tendencies sometimes observed in professional contexts
in India, not a rule that applies to every individual. Individual preferences always
take precedence — see DOC-COMM-001.*

- **Directness:** May be preferred in some professional contexts to soften direct
  requests with more relational framing ("Would it be possible to..." / "When you get a
  chance...") even when the underlying need is time-sensitive — the urgency may not
  always be explicit in the phrasing itself.
- **Formality:** Titles and hierarchy may be acknowledged somewhat more visibly in
  written communication with senior stakeholders than in some other contexts, though
  this varies significantly by individual and by company tenure.
- **Disagreement:** May sometimes be communicated less directly upward in a hierarchy,
  particularly in group settings; a private follow-up conversation can sometimes surface
  a concern that wasn't raised in a group call.
- **Feedback:** May be appreciated when framed constructively alongside acknowledgment of
  what's working, though this varies by individual and by how long someone has worked in
  an international team environment.
- **Urgency:** A stated deadline is still the most reliable way to communicate urgency
  clearly, since relational phrasing can sometimes obscure time-sensitivity.
- **Meetings:** Punctuality expectations align with general company norms; some
  colleagues may be more comfortable contributing in follow-up written comments than
  live in a large group call, particularly early in their tenure.

## What May Unintentionally Land Wrong

- **Too indirect:** A softened request with no explicit deadline may genuinely not be
  understood as urgent, even if that was the intent.
- **Too direct:** Blunt criticism delivered in front of a group, without private
  follow-up, may land harder than intended, regardless of cultural background.
- **Appropriate example:** "Could you take a look at the report and send an update by
  Thursday? Happy to help if anything's unclear."

## Individual Preference Reminder
Bengaluru is Nexora's largest single-office location and includes people with a wide
range of personal communication styles, tenure levels, and prior international work
experience. Treat this profile as a starting hypothesis, not a conclusion about any one
colleague.
