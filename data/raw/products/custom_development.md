# Custom Development Services

For customers whose requirements fall outside the standard product configuration
options, Nexora offers scoped custom development engagements — typically bespoke
reports, custom connectors, or workflow logic that cannot be achieved through standard
configuration or NexAuto.

## Engagement Model

Custom development is quoted separately from standard licensing (see PROC-SALES-002)
and staffed from a dedicated capacity pool within Engineering, not from product squad
capacity. A Solutions Architect scopes the engagement before an Account Executive quotes
it.

## Constraints

Custom development work is billed at a fixed project rate and does not receive ongoing
product-roadmap investment; customers are informed that custom features may require
paid maintenance if a future core product update affects them.
