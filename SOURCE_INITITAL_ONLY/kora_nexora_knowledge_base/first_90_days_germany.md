# First-90-Days Practical Checklist — Germany

Beyond week one, here's a general pace to aim for. Individual circumstances vary — this
is a guide, not a deadline enforced by Nexora.

**By end of week 2:**
- Address registration underway or complete
- Bank account opened

**By end of month 1:**
- Health insurance confirmed
- Tax ID received (usually arrives automatically after address registration —
  check current official guidance if it hasn't arrived within a few weeks)
- Home internet ordered

**By end of month 2:**
- Registered with a local GP
- Comfortable with the public transport routes you use regularly
- Aware of the building/local recycling and waste sorting system

**By end of month 3 (90 days):**
- Settled enough into daily logistics that "settling in" tasks are no longer taking up
  significant weekly mental space
- Have a sense of common German workplace communication norms (see
  germany_workplace_tone.md) and how they compare to what you're used to

If something on this list feels stuck or confusing at any point, your HR Generalist is a
better resource than guessing — and for anything with legal, tax, or immigration
specifics, check the current official guidance for your situation.
