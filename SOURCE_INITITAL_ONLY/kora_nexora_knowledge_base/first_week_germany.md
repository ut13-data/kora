# First-Week Essentials — Germany

A short list, not the full checklist (see germany_settling_in_checklist.md for detail).
Meant to reduce overwhelm — just the things worth prioritizing in week one.

1. Confirm your housing situation and move-in date, if not already settled.
2. Begin the address registration process as soon as you have a fixed address —
   check current official guidance for the exact steps and timing where it applies.
3. Get a local SIM card (a prepaid option is usually fastest).
4. Locate the nearest grocery store, pharmacy, and public transport stop to where you're
   staying.
5. Say hello to your HR Generalist and ask any question, however small — that's what
   this program is for.

You don't need to remember everything at once. This list is intentionally short.
