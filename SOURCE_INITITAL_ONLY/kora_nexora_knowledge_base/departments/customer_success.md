# Customer Success Department

Customer Success owns the ongoing relationship with a customer after go-live: renewal,
expansion, health monitoring, and executive escalations. Based in Austin (LOC-AUS).

## Roles

**Customer Success Manager (CSM)** — Owns a book of ~15–20 customer accounts, runs
quarterly business reviews, and is the primary point of contact for account health.

**CS Ops Analyst** — Maintains the customer health-score model and renewal forecasting
data, working closely with Data & Analytics.

**VP Customer Success** — Owns retention and expansion revenue targets, is the final
internal escalation point for at-risk accounts before executive involvement (see
PROC-CS-001).

## Handoff from Implementation

A CSM is assigned to every account at go-live. The Implementation Manager and incoming
CSM hold a formal handoff call; open items are logged in the account record. See
PROC-IMPL-001, "Handoff to Customer Success."
