# Implementation / Consulting Department

Implementation owns everything between "contract signed" and "customer live in
production," and remains the internal point of contact for complex configuration
changes after go-live. Based in Munich (LOC-MUC), traveling occasionally to customer
sites in DACH and UK.

## Roles

**Implementation Consultant** — Executes customer onboarding projects day-to-day:
configuration, data migration support, and training delivery.

**Solutions Architect** — Owns technical design for complex or multi-product
implementations; escalation point for Implementation Consultants on integration
questions involving NexConnect.

**Implementation Manager** — Owns the onboarding project plan, is the final approval
authority for go-live sign-off (PROC-IMPL-001). This title replaced "Project Lead" as of
the 2025-03-01 reorganization; both terms refer to the same role.

## Notes

Implementation does not own ongoing day-to-day support after go-live — that transfers to
Customer Success (relationship) and Technical Support (issues). See PROC-IMPL-001,
"Handoff to Customer Success."
