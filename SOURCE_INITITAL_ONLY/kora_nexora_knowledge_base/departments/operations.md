# Operations Department

Operations is a small (5-person) team based in Munich (LOC-MUC) that owns office
facilities across all four locations, vendor relationships not owned by IT or Finance,
and general internal process improvement.

## Roles

**Operations Analyst** — Supports internal process documentation, vendor coordination,
and cross-department reporting requests.

**Office Manager** — Owns day-to-day office facilities at a given location (note:
Austin, Bengaluru and London each have a locally-based Office Manager or equivalent
contracted facilities contact; Munich's Office Manager is a direct Nexora employee).

**Operations Manager** — Owns the department's priorities, works closely with Finance
on procurement (PROC-FIN-002) and with IT on office technology rollouts.
