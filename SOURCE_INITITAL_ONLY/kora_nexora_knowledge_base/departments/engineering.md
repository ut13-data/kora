# Software Engineering Department

Engineering is Nexora's largest department (34 people) and is concentrated in
Bengaluru (LOC-BLR), with a secondary pod in Berlin (LOC-BER) owning NexConnect and
platform infrastructure. Organized into four product squads (NexView, NexFlow, NexCRM,
NexAuto) plus a Platform squad (NexConnect, shared infrastructure).

## Roles

**Software Engineer** — Builds and maintains features within a squad, following
PROC-ENG-001.

**Senior Software Engineer** — Same scope with additional responsibility for code
review, technical design, and mentoring.

**Engineering Manager** — Manages one squad (6–9 engineers), owns delivery timelines
and is the escalation point for P1/P2 defects assigned to their squad.

**QA Engineer** — Owns test planning and release verification for a squad.

**DevOps Engineer** — Owns CI/CD pipelines, production infrastructure, and incident
response tooling; primary responder for infrastructure-level incidents (PROC-IT-002).

**VP Engineering** — Reports to CTO, owns engineering headcount and delivery
predictability across all squads.

## Notes

Each squad ships on a two-week sprint cadence. Cross-squad dependencies are tracked in
the internal roadmap tool; Platform squad changes require a 1-week notice period to
dependent squads.
