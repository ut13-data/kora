# Technical Support Department

Technical Support handles customer-reported issues (tickets) after go-live, following a
tiered escalation model. Based in Bengaluru (LOC-BLR) to provide extended-hours coverage
across time zones, with on-call rotation extending into European and US business hours.

## Roles

**Support Engineer L1** — First response, known-issue resolution, ticket triage.

**Support Engineer L2** — Deeper product troubleshooting, configuration-level fixes,
escalation point for L1.

**Support Engineer L3** — Handles the most complex cases, works directly with
Engineering on suspected product defects (see PROC-ENG-002, Bug Reporting &
Escalation).

**Support Team Lead** — Owns ticket queue health, SLA compliance, and staffing;
escalation point for P1 incidents outside business hours.

## Relationship to Engineering

Support does not have write access to production code. Confirmed defects are logged by
an L3 Support Engineer into the Engineering defect tracker per PROC-ENG-002; Support
retains ownership of customer communication throughout.
