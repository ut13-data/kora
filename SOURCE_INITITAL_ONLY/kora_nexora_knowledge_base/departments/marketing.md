# Marketing Department

Marketing is responsible for generating qualified pipeline for Sales and maintaining
Nexora's positioning in the pharmaceutical distribution, manufacturing and retail
verticals. Based primarily in London (LOC-LON), with one remote Content Marketing
Manager based in Munich.

## Roles

**Marketing Specialist** — Executes campaigns (email, webinar, paid) across channels.

**Content Marketing Manager** — Owns case studies, product content, and the company
blog; works closely with Customer Success to source customer stories.

**Demand Generation Manager** — Owns the inbound lead pipeline and lead-scoring model
feeding into PROC-SALES-001 (Lead Qualification).

**VP Marketing** — Owns brand, positioning, and marketing budget; reports directly to
CEO.

## Notes

Marketing-qualified leads (MQLs) are handed to the SDR team via the CRM (NexCRM
internally, "dogfooded" — Nexora runs its own CRM product for its own sales pipeline).
