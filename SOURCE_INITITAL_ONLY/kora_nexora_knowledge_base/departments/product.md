# Product Department

Product owns the roadmap and feature specification for all five Nexora products
(NexView Analytics, NexFlow, NexCRM, NexAuto, NexConnect). Based in Munich (LOC-MUC),
working closely with Engineering (Bengaluru) across time zones — the two teams overlap
roughly during "core hours" of 13:00–16:00 CET / 17:30–20:30 IST.

## Roles

**Product Manager** — Owns roadmap and requirements for one or two products; writes
specs consumed by Engineering in PROC-ENG-001.

**Product Analyst** — Supports Product Managers with usage data analysis, competitive
research, and customer feedback synthesis; works closely with Data & Analytics.

**VP Product** — Owns overall product strategy, chairs the quarterly roadmap review.

## Notes

Product does not own customer-specific configuration — that responsibility sits with
Implementation (see DOC-DEPT-IMPL). Product owns what the product *can* do; Implementation
owns how a specific customer's tenant is *configured* to use it.
