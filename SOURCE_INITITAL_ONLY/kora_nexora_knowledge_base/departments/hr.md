# HR Department

HR is based in Munich (LOC-MUC) and supports all four office locations, working with
local relocation partners for international hires joining the Germany offices.

## Roles

**Recruiter** — Owns sourcing and hiring process for open roles.

**HR Generalist** — Handles day-to-day employee queries: leave, benefits, onboarding
logistics.

**HR Business Partner (HRBP)** — Partners with department heads on org design,
performance processes, and sensitive employee matters.

**HR Manager** — Owns HR policy content and compliance; approval authority for policy
exceptions.

**Head of HR** — Owns overall people strategy, reports to CEO.

## New Joiner Care

HR also owns the **New Joiner Life & Settling In** program (see new_joiner_care.md and
germany_settling_in_checklist.md) for employees relocating internationally to join
Nexora's German offices. This is separate from, and in addition to, standard employee
onboarding (PROC-HR-001).
