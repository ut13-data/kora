# Finance Department

Finance is based in Munich (LOC-MUC) and owns billing, payroll coordination (with HR),
expense processing, procurement, and financial reporting.

## Roles

**AP/AR Specialist** — Processes accounts payable and receivable, including customer
invoicing per PROC-FIN-003.

**Accountant** — Owns month-end close and general ledger accuracy.

**Financial Analyst** — Supports budgeting, forecasting, and ad hoc financial analysis
for department heads.

**Finance Manager** — Approves expenses and purchase orders above standard employee
limits (see PROC-FIN-001, PROC-FIN-002).

**CFO** — Owns company financial strategy and reporting; final approval authority for
purchases above €10,000.

## Note on "Account"

Finance's use of "account" refers to a billable customer record in the billing system,
which is the formal system of record for contract value — see glossary.md, TN-004.
