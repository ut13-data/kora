# NexCRM

Customer relationship management platform covering lead tracking, pipeline management,
and quoting. Nexora's own Sales department uses NexCRM internally ("dogfooding").

## Core Capabilities

- Lead capture and scoring (feeds PROC-SALES-001)
- Pipeline/deal stage tracking
- Quote and proposal generation (feeds PROC-SALES-002)
- Email and calendar integration
- Basic marketing automation (email sequences)

## Typical Customer Profile

Small-to-mid-sized B2B sales teams who find Salesforce or HubSpot too complex or costly
for their team size, particularly in manufacturing and distribution verticals where
Nexora already has a relationship via NexFlow or NexView.

## Cross-Sell Note

NexCRM is most commonly an expansion sale to an existing NexFlow or NexView customer,
handled by the customer's Customer Success Manager in partnership with an Account
Executive.
