# NexFlow

Inventory and operations management software: purchase orders, warehouse stock levels,
batch/lot tracking (important for pharmaceutical customers), and reorder-point
automation.

## Core Capabilities

- Multi-warehouse stock tracking
- Batch/lot and expiry tracking (pharma/food-adjacent customers)
- Purchase order generation and approval workflows
- Reorder point and safety stock alerts
- Integration with NexAuto for automated reorder workflows

## Typical Customer Profile

Distributors and manufacturers who currently run inventory in spreadsheets or an
outdated legacy ERP module. NexFlow is frequently sold alongside NexView Analytics as a
"see your operations, then act on them" bundle.

## Configuration Note

Batch/lot tracking must be enabled at tenant setup — it cannot currently be retroactively
enabled without a data migration project. Implementation Consultants must confirm this
requirement with the customer during discovery, before go-live.
