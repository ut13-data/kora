# Implementation & Support Services

Every Nexora contract includes an Implementation phase (see PROC-IMPL-001) and ongoing
Support access (see PROC-SUP-001) as standard. Premium support tiers (faster SLA,
dedicated Support Engineer) are available as a paid add-on.

## Standard Support Tiers

| Tier | Response SLA (P1) | Included With |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 4 business hours | All contracts |
| Priority | 1 hour, 24/7 | Paid add-on |

## Notes

Implementation Services are one-time (project-based); Support Services are ongoing
(subscription-included). The distinction matters for Finance's billing categorization —
see PROC-FIN-003.
