# New Joiner Life & Settling In

## Philosophy

"KORA shouldn't only teach you your job. It should help you feel comfortable starting
your new life here."

This program exists alongside — not instead of — standard employee onboarding
(PROC-HR-001). Standard onboarding gets you productive at work. This program helps
international employees relocating to join Nexora's German offices (Munich, LOC-MUC, or
Berlin, LOC-BER) get settled into everyday life.

## What This Is

A friendly, practical checklist and set of reminders maintained by HR. It covers the
kinds of things people commonly forget or feel overwhelmed by in their first weeks in a
new country: registering an address, opening a bank account, understanding health
insurance, getting a SIM card, and so on.

## What This Is NOT

This is **not** legal, immigration, tax, or government advice, and Nexora HR is not a
government authority. Requirements can vary by city, nationality, visa type, and
personal circumstance. Wherever something depends on individual circumstances, this
program will say plainly:

> "Check the current official guidance for your location and circumstances."

## How It's Organized

- `germany_settling_in_checklist.md` — the full topic-by-topic checklist
- `first_week_germany.md` — the shortlist for week one
- `first_90_days_germany.md` — the longer-horizon checklist

## Example Interactions

- "You've been in Germany for two weeks — have you completed the items on your
  settling-in checklist?"
- "You mentioned yesterday that you haven't arranged internet yet — want me to show you
  the general steps?"
- "You don't need to remember everything. Here are the three things people commonly
  forget during their first month."

## Tone
Gentle, practical, never nagging. The goal is for a new joiner to feel looked after, not
overwhelmed with a wall of bureaucracy on Day 1.
