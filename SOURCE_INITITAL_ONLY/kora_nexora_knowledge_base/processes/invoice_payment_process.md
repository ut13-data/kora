# Invoice and Payment Process

## Customer Invoicing (Accounts Receivable)
1. Contract terms (from signed SOW) are entered into the billing system at deal close,
   creating the formal **customer account** record (see glossary.md TN-004 — this is the
   "account" in Finance's sense, distinct from "client" in Sales' sense).
2. Invoices are generated per the contract's billing cadence (monthly, quarterly, or
   annual — most Nexora contracts are annual, paid upfront).
3. AP/AR Specialist monitors payment status; overdue invoices (30+ days) trigger an
   automatic reminder, then escalation to the account's Customer Success Manager at
   45 days.

## Vendor Payments (Accounts Payable)
1. Vendor invoices are matched against the originating purchase order (PROC-FIN-002).
2. Approved invoices are paid on Nexora's standard 30-day payment terms unless a vendor
   contract specifies otherwise.

## Dispute Handling
Billing disputes are logged by the AP/AR Specialist and routed to the Finance Manager;
disputes involving contract interpretation are routed jointly to the Customer Success
Manager and Finance Manager.
