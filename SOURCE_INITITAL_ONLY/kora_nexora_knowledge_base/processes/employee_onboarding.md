# Employee Onboarding Process (Employee Onboarding — see glossary.md TN-002 for
disambiguation from Customer Onboarding)

## Purpose
Get a new hire productive and compliant within their first weeks.

## Steps

1. **Pre-start** — HR Generalist sends welcome pack and Day-1 logistics; IT Support
   Specialist provisions standard access (email, chat, HR portal — see PROC-IT-001) to
   be ready before Day 1.
2. **Day 1** — Hiring Manager and HR Generalist run orientation; new hire completes
   employment paperwork.
3. **First week** — New hire completes department-specific onboarding tasks assigned by
   Hiring Manager; for employees relocating to Germany, HR also shares the New Joiner
   Life & Settling In materials (see new_joiner_care.md).
4. **Security training** — **New hires must complete mandatory security awareness
   training within the first 10 working days of their start date.** (See Version
   History Note below regarding a discrepancy with an older document.)
5. **30-day check-in** — HR Generalist holds a structured check-in with the new hire and
   Hiring Manager.
6. **90-day review** — Hiring Manager completes a formal 90-day performance check-in.

## VERSION HISTORY NOTE — Known Conflict
An earlier version of new-hire onboarding guidance (see
`archived_new_hire_security_training_v1_ARCHIVED.md`, Version 1.0, effective 2022-08-01)
stated that security training must be completed **"within 30 days"** of start date. This
current document (Version 3.0, effective 2025-06-01) supersedes that guidance with the
**10 working days** requirement above. The 2025-06-01 version is current; the 30-day
figure should not be used going forward, but it may still appear in older printed
materials or an employee's memory of what they were told during a hiring process before
mid-2025.
