# Lead Qualification Process

## Purpose
Determine which inbound/outbound leads warrant Account Executive time.

## Steps

1. **Lead capture** — Marketing-qualified leads (MQLs) enter NexCRM automatically from
   web forms, webinar signups, and outbound campaigns.
2. **Initial scoring** — Demand Generation Manager's lead-scoring model assigns a score
   0–100 based on company size, industry, and engagement behavior.
3. **SDR triage** — Leads scoring above 60 are assigned to an SDR within 1 business day.
4. **Discovery call** — SDR runs a 15-minute qualification call using the standard
   BANT-style discovery script (Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline).
5. **Qualification decision** — SDR marks the lead as Sales Qualified Lead (SQL) or
   Disqualified, with a reason code.
6. **Handoff to AE** — SQLs are assigned to an Account Executive based on territory
   (geography) and, for larger deals, industry specialization.

## SLA
SDR must action every MQL within 1 business day of assignment. Leads unactioned after 3
business days are automatically re-queued and flagged to the Sales Manager.
