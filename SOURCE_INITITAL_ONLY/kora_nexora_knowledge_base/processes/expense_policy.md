# Expense Policy

## Standard Employee Expense Limits
Employees may submit business expenses (travel, client meals, small tools/subscriptions
under €100) via the expense portal without pre-approval, up to €500/month.

## Approval Thresholds
- Under €500/month cumulative: no pre-approval required, manager reviews on submission.
- €500–€2,000: requires manager pre-approval before booking/purchase where practical.
- Above €2,000: requires Finance Manager approval regardless of category.

## Travel
Standard class flights and 3-star-equivalent accommodation are the default expectation;
exceptions require manager approval in advance.

## Reimbursement Timeline
Approved expenses are reimbursed in the next payroll cycle following approval, typically
within 10 business days.
