# Customer Escalation Policy

## Purpose
Define how at-risk or unhappy customer situations are escalated internally.

## Escalation Levels

1. **Level 1 — CSM-handled** — Standard account concerns (usage questions, minor
   dissatisfaction) handled directly by the assigned Customer Success Manager.
2. **Level 2 — VP Customer Success** — Escalate when: a customer explicitly threatens
   churn, requests a call with Nexora leadership, or an unresolved P1 incident has
   affected the account for more than 24 hours.
3. **Level 3 — Executive (CEO/CTO)** — Reserved for strategic accounts, contract-value
   accounts above €100,000 ARR, or reputational-risk situations (e.g., public complaint,
   data exposure incident).

## Relationship to Incident Management
A technical P1 incident (PROC-SUP-002) affecting a customer does not automatically
trigger a Level 2 customer escalation — the CSM makes that judgment based on customer
relationship context and incident duration. This is an area where individual CSM
judgment matters more than a strict rule.
